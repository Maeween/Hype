Your competitions in Hype: results, track record and championship points

No more scattered results: in Hype, every horse has its own track record, built from its real FFE results. Follow your outings, your championship points and the performances of your whole stable.

Import your FFE results

Give Hype the PDF of your horse's results: every line is read (show, class, placing, number of starters, clear round) and you choose what to keep. The horse's official pedigree, printed on the document, completes its profile along the way.

A track record that builds itself

Outings, wins, podiums and placings are counted for you, season by season. Highlights such as a championship, a final or a major show are put forward, and clear rounds are flagged.

Your championship points

Championship qualifying points are picked up from your results and counted for you, so you know where you stand before the big events.

Start lists

A tool to read competition start lists and find your way on the day.

Your stable's performances

Your stable's “Competition performances” page gathers the results of its riders and horses, from the most recent. Clubs also have their own ranking in the Hype community.

And the results you add yourself

A show that isn't in the document? Add it by hand: it carries a small feather to set it apart from imported results.

Frequently asked questions

Which results can I import?

Your horse's French Equestrian Federation (FFE) results, from the PDF of its results. You can also add a result by hand.

Can everyone see my results?

Results may appear on the horse's public page, without any rider's name. Each rider can hide a result, which then no longer appears anywhere in public.

Is Hype linked to the FFE?

No. Hype is an independent app: it reads the results documents you give it, but has no official link with the French Equestrian Federation.
