Horse health and care: Hype's health record

Vaccinations, worming, farrier, dentist, vet visits: in Hype, every horse has its own health page. See at a glance what has been done, what's coming up soon and who looks after it.

Every due date at a glance

Each horse's vaccination, worming, farrier and dentist dates are gathered in one place. A due date turns orange fifteen days before and red once it's overdue, so a vaccination booster never slips through.

Reminders, right in your calendar

For each type of care, choose to be reminded a few days before and on the day itself. Reminders can also be added to your phone's calendar, with the horse's name in the title.

Each horse's care team

Vet, farrier, equine dentist, osteopath… Each horse's professionals and their contact details are kept on its page. When you add a professional, you can add them to all your horses at once.

Prescriptions and care history

Each treatment keeps its date, notes and the vet's prescriptions. Easily find the last worming, the last shoeing or the treatment given last winter.

Shared with the horse's riders

The owner keeps the page up to date, and the riders who ride the horse can view it. Everyone knows where things stand without having to ask.

The whole stable's health

The stable's health page brings together the care of all its horses, like a yard register: the manager can see at a glance which horse has something due.

And Hey Baby for your questions

Unsure about a treatment or something you've noticed? From the health page, you can ask Hey Baby, Hype's equestrian AI, for advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

You can create your account and your horses' profiles for free. Recording treatments and professionals and receiving reminders requires a Hype+ subscription.

Who can see my horse's care?

The owner and the riders linked to the horse. Riders can view the page; only the owner edits it.

Does Hype replace my horse's passport or official requirements?

No. Hype helps you track and find your horse's care, but it does not replace the horse's identification document or any paperwork required by law. For any health question, your vet's advice remains essential.

Hype is an independent app with no official link to the French Equestrian Federation (FFE). Health information recorded in Hype does not replace a vet's advice.
