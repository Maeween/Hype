Your horse's photos, memories and videos

In Hype, all your horse's pictures finally have a home: its albums, your best moments, its competition videos. Find them in a tap and share them with the other riders who ride it.

Your horse's albums

Sort its photos into albums with the place and date: a competition season, a clinic, its first months. Each album can stay private or be shared.

The memory wall

Recent moments, memories through the years and your horse's whole story in pictures: the Photos page brings back your best moments.

Competition and training videos

Keep its rounds and schooling sessions. A video can last up to three minutes: enough for the entry, the round and the lap of honour.

Highlights on its profile

For each highlight of its track record, choose the photo or video that tells it best. Your horse's profile becomes its story.

Stories ready to share

Put together a story with your photos and a Hype design: the app gives you an image ready to post on Instagram or send on WhatsApp.

One profile for all its riders

Owner, sharer, competition rider: everyone adds their own photos. Each rider's shared albums appear on the horse's profile, while private albums stay personal.

Frequently asked questions

Can everyone see my photos?

No, you decide. A private album stays personal; a shared album appears on the horse's profile for its riders.

Who can add photos to a horse's profile?

The riders linked to the horse. Each person manages their own photos and can remove them at any time.

Is it free?

You can start for free. A Hype+ subscription lets you add more photos and videos.

Hype is an independent app with no official link to the French Equestrian Federation (FFE).
