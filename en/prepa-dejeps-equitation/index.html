DE prep — DEJEPS equestrian sports

The DEJEPS at a glance

The DEJEPS in equestrian sports makes you a state-qualified coach. It is a Ministry of Sport qualification at level 5 (bac + 2, two years of higher education). It lets you teach riding for pay and help horse-and-rider combinations progress in competition, up to Amateur level.

The pathway has three stages, all explained below:

  1. The entry tests, to get a place on the course.
  2. A safety check before you teach on your own on placement.
  3. The 4 exams of the qualification, called UC, which you pass one by one at an approved training centre, often alongside work at a riding centre.

Some exams are on foot, others mounted: each section says which.
