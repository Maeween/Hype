Map of the Parc Équestre Fédéral in Lamotte-Beuvron

2026 edition — this information will be updated for the next edition.

Every summer, the Generali Open de France brings thousands of club and pony riders to the French Federation's equestrian park in Lamotte-Beuvron, in the Sologne region, for the French championships. Here is the map of the arenas and the useful information to find your way.

Map of the arenas

The map shows the park's entrances, competition and warm-up arenas, stabling areas, car parks, and the walking and riding routes. Tap the map to open it full size.

The park entrance

The Parc Équestre Fédéral is in Lamotte-Beuvron, in the Loir-et-Cher department (41), France.

The Warm Up

The Warm Up lets riders train in competition conditions before their classes, in show jumping, dressage, carousel, cross-country or pony games. Slots are booked in advance on the French Equestrian Federation's official website (2026 edition information).

Highlights

Opening ceremony, the Puissance (high-wall) class, the champions' evening: the big moments of the Open de France.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Parc Équestre Fédéral?

In Lamotte-Beuvron, in the Loir-et-Cher department (41), in the Sologne region of France.

When is the Open de France?

Every year in July. In 2026, it took place from 11 to 26 July.

Is this information official?

No. This page was prepared by Hype from the 2026 edition. For timetables, entries and rules, always refer to the organiser's official information.

Hype is an independent app with no official link to the French Equestrian Federation (FFE) or to the organiser of the Open de France.
