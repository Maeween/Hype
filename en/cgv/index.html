2. Purpose

Hype is an app for riders: Galop courses and quizzes, equestrian passport, community, horse tracking and the “Hey Baby” artificial intelligence coach. Some features are free; others are reserved for Premium subscribers. These terms describe the paid offers, their price, payment, duration and cancellation.

4. Order and payment

  • A Hype account is required: the subscription is activated on the account signed in at the time of payment. Before paying, the app shows the account's email address and asks you to confirm it.
  • Payment is made by card on Stripe's secure page. Hype never has access to card numbers.
  • For monthly offers, payment is taken at sign-up and then every month on the same date, until cancellation.
  • If a monthly payment fails, Premium access may be suspended until it is settled.

5. Immediate access and right of withdrawal

The offers give access to digital content as soon as you pay. Before paying, the app asks you to confirm that you want this immediate access and that you therefore waive your 14-day right of withdrawal (article L221-28, 13° of the French Consumer Code). Without this confirmation, payment does not open.

6. Duration, renewal and cancellation

  • Monthly offers (Premium Rider, Premium Family, Duo Pack, Hype AI): no commitment. You can cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the month already paid, and access remains open until then. No started month is refunded.
  • Cercle Crystal: 12 months from payment, then access ends by itself. Nothing further is charged.
  • How to cancel: online, at any time, with the “Manage my subscription” button in My account (Stripe's secure page, where you can also change your card and find your invoices). You can also write to feinn@live.fr from your account's email address.
  • If you delete your Hype account while a subscription is running, remember to cancel it: deleting the account does not automatically stop payments. For Premium Family, the holder must cancel first.

7. Premium Family rules

  • The person who pays is the holder. They invite a second rider from My account, with a link valid for 7 days.
  • Each keeps their own account, data and 4 AI questions a day; nothing is shared between the two accounts.
  • The holder can remove the second rider, and the second rider can leave the family. A replacement is possible at most once every 30 days, with at most 4 different second riders per year.
  • No family relationship is required. The holder is responsible for choosing the invited person.
  • If the subscription ends (cancellation, failed payment), Premium access ends for both accounts.

8. Hype patronage

Patronage tiers (from €20 to €500) are a voluntary one-off support for the app's development. They do not give access to Premium, are not a donation to the FEINN association and give no tax deduction. They are not refundable, except for an obvious error reported promptly to feinn@live.fr.

9. Service operation

The association does its best to keep Hype running continuously, but interruptions can happen (maintenance, provider outage). The AI coach's answers are general advice produced by artificial intelligence: they replace neither a qualified instructor nor a vet, and may contain errors.

10. Personal data

The data needed for the account and subscriptions is processed to provide the service. You can request access to your data or its deletion by writing to feinn@live.fr.

Photos and videos. By posting a photo or video on Hype, you agree to respect the people who appear in it and, for a minor, to have their parents' consent. Anyone who appears in a photo or video can ask for it to be removed from “My account › Request a photo removal” or by writing to feinn@live.fr; the request is handled as quickly as possible.

11. Complaints and mediation

For any complaint, write first to feinn@live.fr. Consumer mediator: being appointed; their details will be given here. In the meantime, any complaint can be sent to feinn@live.fr. You can also use the European online dispute resolution platform.

12. Applicable law

These terms are governed by French law. They may be updated; the applicable version is the one in force on the day of payment.
