Your stable, its calendar, messages and groups

Hype brings your stable's life together in one place: the club page, its events, conversations between riders and everyone's photos. No more juggling several apps to find out who is coming to Sunday's show.

Your stable's page

Photo, quote, contact details, the club's horses and upcoming events: your stable's page introduces your club to riders, and to visitors looking for it on Google.

The club calendar

Shows, clinics, rides, parties: every event has its own page. Riders answer « I'm coming » and add it to their phone's calendar in one tap. Past events stay available on their own page.

Messaging and groups

Write to a rider, or create a group: the competition team, the Wednesday riders, the owners. An event can also appear in the groups concerned, with the horses that are going.

The stable's albums

The club's photos, and private albums shared with a group where every member adds their own: the forest ride, the summer clinic, the championship.

Alerts on your phone

Add Hype to your home screen and get an alert when something happens at your stable: you won't miss a thing.

The competition team and the club ranking

Follow your team's results and see where your club stands in the club ranking, based on the points earned at shows.

Do you run a stable?

Claim your stable's page from the app. Your request is checked before approval, for example by calling the stable. Once accepted, you manage the page's photo, quote, calendar and contact details.

Frequently asked questions

Is my stable already on Hype?

More than 3,000 stables and riding schools in France already have their page. Look for yours in the app.

How do I become the manager of my stable's page?

Open your stable's page in Hype and tap « Claim this page ». The request is checked, then you can manage the page.

Do events go into my phone's calendar?

Yes: on each event, tap « Add to your calendar » to save it in your phone's calendar.

Hype is an independent app with no official link to the French Equestrian Federation (FFE).
