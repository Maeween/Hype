Hey Baby, Hype's equestrian AI

Hey Baby is Hype's artificial intelligence assistant, designed for riders. It answers your riding questions, looks at your photos and helps you progress, ride after ride.

Ask all your riding questions

Position, aids, gaits, horse care, preparing for a level test or a competition: ask in your own words and Hey Baby answers simply.

  • “Why does my horse stiffen in the canter transition?”
  • “How can I work on my seat without stirrups?”
  • “What do I need to know for my next level?”

It remembers you and your horse

Hey Baby keeps the history of your conversations and knows your horse. Its advice takes into account what you have already worked on together, instead of starting from scratch with every question.

It analyses your photos

Send a photo of yourself riding or of your horse: Hey Baby looks at position, balance and posture, and tells you what is going well and what can be improved. For example, it checks whether your centre of gravity and your horse's are aligned: a rider leaning too far forward overloads the forehand, a rider sitting too far back makes the horse hollow its back.

Your advice, kept in your logbook

The advice that matters to you is kept in My logbook, linked to your horse and your rides. Mark it “to work on”, “in progress” or “achieved” and follow its history over the weeks.

Guidelines written by a riding instructor

The way Hey Baby analyses and advises is defined and checked by a qualified riding instructor. It sticks to what matters: when there is nothing to say about a point, it doesn't make something up.

In your language

Hey Baby answers in French, English, Spanish, Italian, German, Japanese or Arabic.

Frequently asked questions

Does Hey Baby replace my instructor or my vet?

No. Hey Baby helps you understand and progress between lessons, but it does not replace your instructor's eye or a vet's opinion. For any health problem or unusual behaviour, ask a professional.

How do I use Hey Baby?

Create your Hype account, then open Hey Baby in the app. You can write to it or send it a photo.

Which languages does it speak?

All 7 Hype languages: French, English, Spanish, Italian, German, Japanese and Arabic.

Hype is an independent app with no official link to the French Equestrian Federation (FFE). Hey Baby is an artificial intelligence assistant: its answers may contain mistakes.
