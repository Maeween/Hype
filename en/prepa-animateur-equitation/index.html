AE prep — Animateur d'Équitation

The AE at a glance

The Animateur d'Équitation (AE, riding leader) is the first qualification for supervising riding. It is an FFE award at level 3 (CAP level, a vocational certificate). It lets you run taster and beginner sessions, under the authority of a qualified instructor (BPJEPS or higher), but not supervise competition at Amateur level or above. You also help with caring for the horses, looking after the yard, welcoming riders and promoting the centre.

A new set of rules has applied since 17 July 2026, and this page follows it. From 1 January 2027, every candidate takes the new exams, even those who started training before.

The pathway has three stages, all explained below:

  1. The entry requirements: your Galop and a few documents. There is no ridden entry test.
  2. A safety check before you teach on placement.
  3. The 3 exams of the qualification, called competence blocks (BC). Each block is passed separately and stays passed.

Training is often work-based. It includes at least 490 hours at the centre and a placement of at least 294 hours at an FFE-accredited riding club (on an apprenticeship, the placement makes up at least 70 % of the contract).
